# TR-40 launch — distributor briefing

Tallvik Components sells into 14 European markets, 9 of them direct and 5 through
distributors.

Net sales were SEK 812 million in the last financial year.

The TR-40 crankset is certified to ISO 4210 and carries third-party certification against EN 14781
certification.

The mechanic training programme certified 4,200 individual mechanics last year, and
the partner portal serves 180 distributor and reseller organisations. The units differ:
4,200 counts people, 180 counts companies.

Customer satisfaction reached 84% in the most recent annual survey.
